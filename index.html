Guides & Insights on Image Compression

Q: WebP format kya hai aur iska website speed par kya asar hota hai?

WebP ek modern web image format hai jo JPEG aur PNG ke muqable 30% se 50% chota size deta hai bina image quality kharab kiye. WebP use karne se website ka load time drastically decrease ho jata hai aur SEO ranking improve hoti hai.

Q: Kya browser mein photo compress karna bilkul safe hai?

Ji haan! Hamara compressor **HTML5 Canvas** technology use karta hai jo compression user ke hi machine/browser par karti hai. Aapki image internet par kisi server par upload nahi hoti, jo ise 100% private banata hai.

Interactive Glassmorphic Cards FAQ

Q: Glassmorphism design system kya hai?

Glassmorphism ek popular graphic layout concept hai jo translucent frosted-glass aesthetic par focused hai. Ye interfaces ko high-end, visual depth aur futuristic glowing look deta hai. Ise achieve karne ke liye standard CSS borders aur `backdrop-filter: blur()` properties utilize hoti hain.

Q: Kya glassmorphism elements har ek browser mein support hote hain?

Ji haan, modern major browsers (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, iOS, Android browsers) `backdrop-filter` property ko support karte hain. Purane browsers ke fallback ke liye hum ek translucent standard background color fallback automatically provide kar dete hain.

Understanding Compound Growth & FIRE

Q: Compound Interest kya hota hai aur ye kaise kaam karta hai?

Compound Interest (murakkab sood) ko Albert Einstein ne dunia ka 8th ajooba kaha tha. Iska asan matlab hai ke aapko apne shuruyati paiso (principal) par toh interest milta hi hai, par sath hi jo interest aap kama chuke hain, us interest par bhi interest milta rehta hai. Ye time ke sath exponentially barhta hai.

Q: FIRE movement kya hai?

FIRE ka matlab hai **Financial Independence, Retire Early** (Mali taur par khudmukhtari aur jald retirement). Is movement ka maqsad hai ke log apni jawani mein zyada se zyada savings aur smart investments karein, taake unka interest investment unke monthly expenses ko cover kar sake aur woh apni job chor kar azaad ho sakein.
